# Phase 3A.3 — retained floor-art prompts

Built-in image-generation mode; no CLI/API fallback. Sources are generation drafts, not native FPGA assets. Final conversion uses nearest-neighbor sampling at 24×24, deterministic contrast adjustment, and an exact RGB332 palette. Generated sources are retained under `source/` so previews regenerate without another image-generation call.

## base_final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: ONE low-resolution 24x24 top-down ancient dark-gray stone FLOOR tile for a retro pixel-art dungeon. Critical: depict a SINGLE LARGE weathered slab filling the entire square, NOT many small stones. No cracks crossing its center, NO moss on this ancient stone variant. EXACTLY 24x24 logical square pixels with very large visible pixels. Opaque image with no border or margin. Subtle irregular chipped perimeter, uneven gently shaded upper and left bevel, small groups of worn mineral chips, shallow weathering depressions. Interior spacious with clustered surface texture, not scattered black dots. Restrained shades: #242455 deep charcoal shadow used sparingly, #494955 dominant gray stone, #6D6D55 muted warm highlights in short edge clusters. No pure black, no yellow, no green, no outlines, no bright highlights. Quiet but convincingly detailed stone relief, professional hand-painted pixel clusters; floor subordinate to colorful sprites. Render flat orthographic, no block sides, no perspective. Avoid high resolution microdetail, noise, regular square frame, multi-slab mosaic, smooth gradients, text, watermarks.
